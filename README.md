Privacy Policy - Crystal Climber
Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Welcome to Crystal Climber ("the Game"), developed by AI Studio. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you play Crystal Climber.

Summary: We collect minimal data necessary for game functionality, such as high scores, unlocked characters, in-app purchases, and Google Play Games achievements. We do not sell your personal data.
1. Information We Collect
When you play Crystal Climber, we may collect and process the following information:

Game Progress & Player Data: High scores (altitude meters), total crystals collected, unlocked characters, equipped skins, auras, and quest achievements.
Authentication Information: Anonymous Firebase User ID or Google Play Games ID (Player ID & Display Name) when you sign in.
In-App Purchase Data: Purchase tokens and order IDs provided by Google Play Billing to verify and grant Void Orbs.
Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, OS version, and crash/performance logs to ensure game stability.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:

To synchronize your game progress, high scores, and inventory across devices via Cloud Firestore.
To display global leaderboards and rank your achievements among other climbers.
To process and fulfill in-app purchases securely via Google Play Billing Client and Cloud Functions.
To detect and prevent cheating or unauthorized modification of game scores.
3. Third-Party Services
The Game integrates with trusted third-party services provided by Google LLC:

Google Play Services & Google Play Games
Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore
Google Play In-App Billing
4. Account & Data Deletion Policy
In compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy, you have full control over your personal data and account:

In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the Game:
Open Settings ➔ Scroll to "Account & Cloud" ➔ Tap "Delete Account & Data" ➔ Confirm.
Request Deletion via Email: You can also request complete manual deletion of your player profile, cloud records, and purchase history by contacting us at vimartu@icloud.com with your Player ID.
Upon deletion, all your profile data, high scores, leaderboard entries, and cloud records stored in Cloud Firestore will be permanently erased.

5. Children's Privacy
Crystal Climber does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate removal.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the app or on our store page.
